Nostalgia

Every agent, one glance away.

Damla shows each Claude Code and Codex session: which project is working, which one is waiting on you, and for how long. When one asks for permission, the exact command drops out of the notch.

  • Allow, Deny, Always allow. For Claude Code and Codex CLI, or ⌃⌥↩ and ⌃⌥⌫ from any app. Try it on the panel.
  • Answer its questions. When Claude Code asks you to choose, choose in the notch.
  • Context and limits. How full each session is, your five-hour and weekly use, and the dev servers running on your Mac.
  • One click in Settings › Agents adds the hooks to Claude Code and Codex, after a backup. They report status only: phase, tool name, timing. Prompts and output are never read.

Notifications drop from the notch.

A message, a mail, a calendar alert: other apps' notifications arrive as a card under the notch instead of a banner in the corner. Click it to reply or use its buttons; ✕ sends it away.

  • Reply where you are. Answer a WhatsApp message without leaving your editor; Open takes you to the conversation. Click the card on the panel.
  • Missed ones wait an hour. The Notifications page stacks the last hour's notifications by app, then lets them go; nothing piles up, and the closed notch stays quiet.
  • Kept in memory only. Nothing is written to disk, and it's gone when Damla quits.

Now playing, from anything.

Apple Music, Spotify, podcasts, YouTube in the browser: cover, progress and controls, with the whole panel tinted by the album you're hearing.

  • Lyrics that scroll with the song. The panel stretches down and the words flow.
  • Video under the notch. A video in Chrome, Brave, Edge or Vivaldi keeps playing under the notch, browser in the background or not.
  • Smart handoff. Start a video and the music pauses; stop it and the music comes back. A paused player leaves the notch after a few seconds.

A shelf for your files.

Start dragging a file anywhere and the notch opens into a drop zone. Preview with Space, drag it out into another app, or AirDrop it. New screenshots land there by themselves.

  • Convert and shrink. Save an image as PNG, JPEG or HEIC, compress it, or merge the PDFs on the shelf into one.
  • Try it here. Drop a file from your Mac onto this page. It stays in your browser.
  • Damla only remembers where your files are. It never moves or changes them.

An instant camera in the notch.

Mirror checks your hair before a call. Press the shutter and it takes exactly what the viewfinder frames, then saves it as a wide Polaroid in Pictures › Damla.

  • A real Polaroid. Cream border, an orange date stamp in the corner and the date written in the margin, if you want it. The newest one waits in the corner.
  • Three films. Nostalgia, black and white, natural. A 3-second timer and Center Stage, always one click away.

No account. No cloud. No tracking.

Everything stays on your Mac. Your shelf, clipboard history and timer live in ~/Library/Application Support/Damla/; notifications and their text are kept in memory only.

Only two things touch the network: lyrics, if you turn them on (title, artist, album and duration go to lrclib.net), and the daily update check, which is counted without storing your IP address or any identifier.
